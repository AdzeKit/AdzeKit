# Capture

**Goal:** turn something messy (a message, meeting notes, a voice memo, a
pasted thread) into the right lines in the right files.

1. Read the input. Pull out, and quote the source for each:
   - **commitments** (who owes what, and by when) go to `loops/active.md`;
   - **progress and decisions** go to a dated entry in the matching
     `projects/<slug>.md` log;
   - **durable facts or ideas** go to the matching `knowledge/<topic>.md`, or
     a new note linked with `[[...]]`.
2. Show me the planned edits as a short list. Apply them once I confirm.
   Small, obvious edits (one loop, one log line) can go straight in.
3. Never create a new project without asking. The active cap applies.
4. Keep raw material out of notes. Save long originals under
   `stock/<slug>/` (not tracked in git) and link to them.
